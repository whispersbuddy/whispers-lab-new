Case study

From 4 hours a day to 3 minutes.

Client type Growth-stage marketing agency
Location Toronto, CA
Team size 12 people
Project duration 3 weeks

THE PROBLEM

The agency was generating 60–80 inbound leads per week across Typeform, Facebook Lead Ads, and direct email. Each lead required a human to: check the source, copy data into their CRM, assign to a sales rep in Slack, add to an email nurture sequence in ActiveCampaign, and log in a Google Sheet. Four team members were spending 45 minutes each morning just on this task.

THE SOLUTION

We built a unified lead intake pipeline in n8n that ingested from all three sources, normalised the data, used OpenAI to score and categorize each lead, created the CRM contact in GoHighLevel, assigned based on territory rules, sent a rich Slack notification to the assigned rep with one-click actions, and enrolled in the correct ActiveCampaign sequence — all in under 10 seconds.

FAQ

Questions about automation we get asked most.

What automation tools do you use?

We primarily build with n8n, Make.com, GoHighLevel, ActiveCampaign, and Zapier. For complex systems we write custom code and use direct API integrations. We'll recommend the right tool for your situation — not the one we're most comfortable with charging you for.

How long does a project take?

Most single-workflow automations are done in 1–2 weeks. Multi-system pipelines with AI layers take 2–4 weeks. We always give you a fixed timeline in the written scope document before starting.

Do I need to be technical to use what you build?

No. We build systems your team can monitor without coding. At handoff, we provide a full Loom walkthrough and written documentation. If something breaks or needs adjusting, you'll know exactly what to look at.

What happens if the automation breaks after you hand it over?

We include a post-launch support window in every project (2 weeks minimum). For ongoing support, we offer a monthly maintenance retainer. We also build proper error handling and alerts into every workflow — so you know immediately if something fails.

Can you automate something we already use Zapier for?

Yes. We frequently migrate Zapier workflows to n8n or Make.com — especially when clients have hit Zapier's task limits, complexity limits, or recurring failure issues. We'll assess whether it's worth migrating during the scoping call.

Do you work with our existing CRM?

Probably yes. We have deep experience with GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, and most other CRMs. If it has an API or a native integration, we can connect to it.

What if our process changes after the automation is built?

Automation workflows are modular by design. Small changes (adding a step, changing a condition) are usually quick and low-cost. Major process changes require a new scope — but we build with change in mind.

Do you offer a retainer for ongoing automation work?

Yes. Our automation retainer covers: monitoring, minor updates, new workflow additions, and priority support. Pricing starts at $400/month. Most clients activate it after their first project.
